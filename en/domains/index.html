Frequently asked questions

What is included in domain registration?

Registration gives you the right to use a domain for the purchased period, subject to the provider's terms. A website, hosting and email are normally separate.

How much does a domain cost?

Prices depend on the name, extension and registration conditions. Check the initial and renewal prices on the platform before paying. Technical support is agreed separately.

Who owns the domain?

Enter the actual holder's details during registration and keep control of your account. Check the registration terms and access arrangements before purchase.

Can I connect the domain to an existing website?

Usually, yes. The necessary DNS settings depend on your website provider. I can help with the configuration for domains registered through MG Webmaster.

Does the domain include an email mailbox?

Not automatically. An email mailbox requires a separate email service and compatible DNS settings.

Can I transfer a domain I already own?

We can check eligibility and the current provider's requirements. Website files and mailboxes do not automatically move with a domain transfer.

What happens when my domain expires?

Renew it according to your provider's terms to keep using it. Check the expiry date, renewal price and automatic renewal settings in your account.

Can you help if the name I want is taken?

Yes. I can help explore alternative names or check whether a domain is offered for sale. Availability, price and any assistance are subject to prior agreement.
