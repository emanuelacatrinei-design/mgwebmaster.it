Despre noi the studio

More Than Photography

A way of seeing the world through people, places, light and honest moments.

We create editorial imagery for brands, people and destinations. The Proiecte moves between portraiture, travel, architecture, automotive and lifestyle photography, always with a cinematic eye and a preference for atmosphere over excess.

The studio is built for collaborations that need more than technically perfect pictures — they need a point of view.

Our philosophy

People. Places. Real Povești.

Good photography feels lived-in. It leaves room for context, imperfection, weather, movement and silence.
